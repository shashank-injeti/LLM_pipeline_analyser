Under the surface — models, mathematics, and accuracy

Token IDs come from gpt-tokenizer using the selected BPE encoding. Counts cover the text only, excluding chat-message overhead. Vocabulary uses locale-aware word segmentation and case-insensitive word counts; lexical diversity is unique words ÷ words.

Each decoded token piece is independently embedded by all-MiniLM-L6-v2 with mean pooling and L2 normalization. These are semantic embeddings of the token text, not GPT's internal embedding weights or contextual hidden states. Very short fragments, whitespace, and punctuation may have limited semantic meaning. Partial UTF-8 tokens use a surrounding decodable span.

Cosine similarity is computed from the original 384-dimensional vectors: cos(a,b) = (a·b) / (‖a‖‖b‖). Scores range from −1 to 1; they are not probabilities. PCA fits three axes to the first sentence. Missing axes for very short inputs are completed with orthogonal directions. Every later sentence and added word uses this fixed center, axes, and display scale, so existing points do not move. The variance percentage describes the first sentence only. Reset space clears all points and lets the next sentence define new axes. A 3D projection loses information: visual distances cannot exactly preserve all cosine similarities. Rankings always use the original vectors. Identical points may overlap.

Processing happens locally in your browser. Model files and JavaScript libraries download from Hugging Face and jsDelivr; your input is not sent to an inference API. Every analysis automatically adds all its tokens to the graph. There is no preset character or token-count limit. Longer inputs take more time and memory; practical capacity depends on your browser and device. The space accumulates sentences and words, and is saved in this browser when local storage is available. Only Reset space clears it.
